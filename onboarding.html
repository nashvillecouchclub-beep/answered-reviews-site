Give us manager access on Google

Takes about 2 minutes. You stay the owner. Please never send us your Google password. We add only one manager account to your profile. You never share your password with us.

  1. On your computer, sign in to the Google account that owns your business profile.
  2. Search Google for your business name, or open Google Maps and go to your listing. You will see your profile tools at the top.
  3. Click the three dot Menu (it may say More), then Business Profile settings.
  4. Click People and access.
  5. Click Add at the top.
  6. Type our email: rickey@answeredreviewsco.com
  7. Under Access, choose Manager. Please do not choose Owner.
  8. Click Invite.

We will accept the invite and email you when we are in. You can take our access away any time from the same People and access screen.
