Client terms

Our terms, in plain words

This page is the whole deal between your business ("you") and Answered Reviews ("we"). Version 2026-10-20.

1. What we do

We draft and post replies to your Google reviews, and do any other work in the plan you pick, as listed on our price page. We post only on Google, as a manager you add. You stay the owner of your profile.

2. Our role: your outside vendor

We are an independent company, not your employee. We act as your vendor and agent, in your business name, only for the work in your plan. You keep your own business and trade licenses. We do not give legal, insurance or hiring advice.

3. On time promise

Replies are posted within 24 hours of your OK (Full and Concierge: 4 hours). In your first month, if a reply we are cleared to post waits more than 2 business days, that month is credited back to you. Replies waiting on your OK do not count.

4. Your OK

Replies to 1 to 3 star reviews wait for your OK. If a hard review waits 48 hours for your OK, we post the safe reply you approved at sign up. You can ask us to hold any reply.

5. Our rules on reviews

  • We never write fake reviews or ask for reviews only from happy customers.
  • We never offer gifts or discounts for reviews.
  • We never share a customer's private details in a reply.
  • We report a review to Google only when it clearly breaks Google's rules.

6. Case studies and references

We may share your results as a case study or ask you to be a reference only if you say yes in writing. You can say no, or change your mind any time.

7. No contract, cancel any time

  • Every plan is month to month.
  • Cancel by email or on the change or cancel page.
  • We stop at the end of your current month and end our manager access within 7 business days.
  • No fee to leave.

8. How you pay

  • You save a card on Square's secure page at sign up. We never see or store your card number.
  • Saving your card costs nothing today. A one time service (Tune Up or Catch Up) is charged once that work is done. Monthly plans: your first fee is charged 30 days after we start, once your first month of replies is done, and then every 30 days.
  • We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge: nothing to pay, the date, and what it covers.
  • Every charge, of any size, goes through automatically. You do not need to approve it.
  • You get a detailed receipt after every charge.
  • If a charge fails, we tell you right away and try again.
