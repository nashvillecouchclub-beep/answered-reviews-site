Client terms

Our terms, in plain words

This page is the whole deal between your business ("you") and Answered Reviews ("we"). Version 2026-10-20.

1. What we do

We draft and post replies to your Google reviews, and do any other work in the plan you pick, as listed on our price page. We post only on Google, as a manager you add. You stay the owner of your profile.

2. We work for you, in your name

We act as your vendor and agent, in your business name, only for the work in your plan. You keep your own business and trade licenses. We do not give legal, insurance or hiring advice.

3. Your OK

Replies to 1 to 3 star reviews wait for your OK. If a hard review waits 48 hours for your OK, we post the safe reply you approved at sign up. You can ask us to hold any reply.

4. Fees

  • Google Profile Tune Up: $49, one time
  • Review Catch Up: $99 (up to 15), $199 (16 to 40), $349 (41 to 100), more than 100 by written quote. Credited to your first month if you start a monthly plan within 30 days, up to that month's price.
  • Replies $79 a month, Replies + Posts $149 a month, Full $299 a month, Reputation Concierge from $599 a month per location by written quote
  • Prepay a year: 2 months free. Prepay 3 months: 10% off. Sign within 7 days of your free replies: half off your first month or first fee. Offers do not stack.

5. How you pay

You save a card on Square's secure page at sign up. We never see or store your card number. Nothing is charged until your first job or first month is delivered. Each charge then happens on its own under these terms. We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge (nothing to pay, the date, and what it covers) and send a detailed receipt after. Every charge, of any size, goes through automatically; you do not need to approve it. If a charge fails, we tell you right away and try again.

6. On time promise

Replies are posted within 24 hours of your OK (Full and Concierge: 4 hours). In your first month, if a reply we are cleared to post waits more than 2 business days, that month is credited back to you. Replies waiting on your OK do not count.

7. Our rules on reviews

  • We never write fake reviews or ask for reviews only from happy customers.
  • We never offer gifts or discounts for reviews.
  • We never share a customer's private details in a reply.
  • We report a review to Google only when it clearly breaks Google's rules.

8. No contract, cancel any time

Every plan is month to month. Cancel by email or on the change or cancel page. We stop at the end of your current month and end our manager access within 7 business days. No fee to leave.

9. Case studies and references

We may share your results as a case study or ask you to be a reference only if you say yes in writing. You can say no, or change your mind any time.
