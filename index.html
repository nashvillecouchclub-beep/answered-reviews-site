Questions

Will the replies really sound like me?

That is the whole job. Before we start, you tell us the words you use, the words you never use, and how you like to sign off. If a reply ever feels off, tell us and we change it.

What happens with a bad review?

We write a calm, honest reply that invites the customer to reach out to you directly, and send it to you first. Nothing is posted on a 1 to 3 star review until you tap OK. We never argue with a customer.

Can you take down bad reviews or get me more stars?

No, and nobody honestly can. Real reviews from real customers stay up. We only report a review to Google when it clearly breaks Google's own rules, like a fake review or one about a different business. What we can do is make sure every reviewer hears back from you, which is what future customers read.

Do you reply on Facebook or Yelp?

We work only on Google. Google allows business owners to add a manager with written permission, so that is where we post. We do not post on Facebook or Yelp for you.

I run a dental, medical or vet practice. Is that OK?

Yes. For health practices, replies never confirm that someone was a patient and never mention a visit, treatment, date or bill. We thank the reviewer and invite them to call the office.

Do you own my Google profile?

No. You stay the owner. We are added as a manager from our own Google account, never with your password. You can take our access away any time from your Google Business Profile settings, and if you ask, we help you do it within 7 business days.

Is this an app?

No. It is a done for you service from a small team. You get replies posted, a monthly report, and a real person to email.

How do I cancel?

Reply to any of our emails and say cancel. We stop at the end of your current month and end our manager access within 7 business days. No contracts and no fees to leave.

Would you rather talk first?

Happy to. Email us and ask for a quick 15 minute call, and we will send a few times that work. Or start with a free audit of your Google reviews.
