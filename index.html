How it works

  1. Pick a plan and sign up.

    Give us your written OK on the sign up form. We email you a QuickBooks invoice, and paying it sets up monthly autopay.

  2. Tell us how you talk.

    A short form asks for words you like, words to avoid, how to sign replies, and who should hear about unhappy reviews. Then you add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile. It takes about two minutes and we walk you through it.

  3. We start replying.

    New reviews get a reply within one business day. You get a summary each month and can change anything any time.

Questions

Will the replies really sound like me?

That is the whole job. Before we start, you tell us the words you use, the words you never use, and how you like to sign off. If a reply ever feels off, tell us and we fix it.

What happens with a bad review?

For any 1 to 3 star review, we let the person you choose know the same day, before anything is posted. We write a calm, honest reply that invites the customer to reach out to you directly. We never argue with a customer.

Can you get me more reviews or move me up in Google?

We cannot promise either, and nobody honestly can. What we can do is make sure every customer who takes the time to write a review hears back from you.

On the Founding Plan and the $400 plan, your team also gets a simple way to ask every customer for a review. We never offer rewards for reviews, never pick only happy customers to ask, and never post fake reviews. Those things break Google's rules and can put your profile at risk.

Do you own my Google profile?

No. You stay the owner. We are added as a manager, which lets us reply and post. You can remove us any time from your Google Business Profile settings.

How do I cancel?

Email us or reply to any of our emails and say cancel. We stop at the end of your current month and remove our access. No contracts and no fees to leave.
