Questions

Will the replies really sound like me?

That is the whole job. Before we start, you tell us the words you use, the words you never use, and how you sign off. If a reply ever feels off, tell us and we change it.

What happens with a bad review?

We write a calm, honest reply that invites the customer to reach out to you, and send it to you first. Nothing is posted on a 1 to 3 star review until you give your one click OK. If it is still waiting on you, we send a friendly reminder email after 24 hours and again after 48 hours. We never argue with a customer.

Can you take down bad reviews or get me more stars?

No, and nobody honestly can. Real reviews from real customers stay up. We only report a review to Google when it clearly breaks Google's own rules, like a fake review or one about a different business. What we can do is make sure every reviewer hears back from you, which is what future customers read.

Do you reply on Facebook or Yelp?

We post only on Google, where owners can add a manager with written permission. On Concierge we send you ready to paste replies for Facebook and Yelp that you post yourself.

Do I need to learn any software?

No software to buy, nothing to learn. Everything happens by email. You get drafts to OK, a monthly report, and a reply to every question.

Do you own my Google profile?

No. You stay the owner. You add us as a manager, and you never share your password. You can take our access away any time, and if you ask, we end it within 7 business days.

Is this done by AI?

We use AI to draft replies, and every reply follows your rules. Hard reviews always wait for your OK. If anyone asks, we tell them how replies are written.

When do I pay?

You save a card on Square's secure page at sign up. Saving your card costs nothing today. A one time service (Tune Up or Catch Up) is charged once that work is done. Monthly plans: your first fee is charged 30 days after we start, once your first month of replies is done, and then every 30 days. We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge, and a receipt after.

How do I cancel?

Reply to any of our emails and say cancel, or use the change or cancel page. We stop at the end of your current month and end our manager access within 7 business days. No fees to leave.
