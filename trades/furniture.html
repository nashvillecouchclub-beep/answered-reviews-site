Questions

Do you work with furniture stores, new and used?

Yes. We answer Google reviews for furniture stores, new and used, in your voice. Healthcare and medical practices are not part of our service right now.

What happens with a bad review?

We write a calm, honest reply that invites the customer to reach out to you, and send it to you first. Nothing is posted on a 1 to 3 star review until you tap OK. We never argue with a customer.

Do I need to learn any software?

No software to buy, nothing to learn. Everything happens by email. You get drafts to OK, a monthly report, and a reply to every question.

Do you own my Google profile?

No. You stay the owner. You add us as a manager, and you never share your password. You can take our access away any time, and if you ask, we end it within 7 business days.

When do I pay?

You save a card on Square's secure page at sign up. Nothing is charged until your first job or first month is done. We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge, and a receipt after.

How do I cancel?

Reply to any of our emails and say cancel, or use the change or cancel page. We stop at the end of your current month and end our manager access within 7 business days. No fees to leave.
